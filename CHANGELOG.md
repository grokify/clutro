## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-14 ([`09b2b3c`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/09b2b3c))

### Highlights

- A pure-Go, zero-dependency clustering toolkit in the CLUTO tradition: sparse and dense matrices, the I2 criterion, repeated bisection with refinement, spherical k-means, cluster explanation, external and internal evaluation metrics, CLUTO file formats and a `vcluster`-compatible CLI, all deterministic for a seed regardless of `GOMAXPROCS`.
- Validated against CLUTO's own outputs on 103 real text corpora: CLUTRO's partitions score 1.0007× (`rb`) to 1.033× (`rbr`) of CLUTO's on the criterion CLUTO optimized; eight numeric-only corpora are published as fixtures with an asserted agreement band.

### Added

- `matrix`: `SparseVec`, the `Rows` interface, `CSR` (from rows or triplets, L2 row normalization, column scaling, document frequencies) and `Dense` with cached row norms, merge-join dot products, and `Composite` cluster vectors with incremental add/remove and what-if norms ([`01f42d6`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/01f42d6))
- `matrix.Preprocess` with `RowL2` and `ColIDF` (CLUTO `-rowmodel`/`-colmodel`) ([`6829e27`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/6829e27))
- `metric`: `Similarity` interface with `Cosine` (normalized fast path) and `Euclidean` (as distance and as similarity, centroid-based against composites) ([`c9cf40f`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/c9cf40f))
- `criterion`: the `Criterion` interface and `I2` (Σ‖D_k‖) with an O(nnz) single-row move delta ([`84f32e6`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/84f32e6))
- `partition`: `KMeans` (greedy k-means++ seeding, Lloyd, spherical under cosine), `Bisect` repeated bisection with `SelectBest`/`SelectLargest` and a bisection `Tree` with `CutMaxSize`/`CutK`, `Refine` (criterion-driven single-row moves) and `Direct`; deterministic empty-cluster reseeding ([`836b62d`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/836b62d))
- `clutro.Fit` facade with functional options (algorithm, clusters, similarity, criterion, refinement, seed, trials, iterations, selection, parallelism, row/column labels) returning a `Result` with labels, sizes, clusters, composites and the bisection tree ([`4d0f588`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/4d0f588))
- `explain`: descriptive and discriminating features, representatives and ISim/ESim summaries, exposed as `Result.Describe`, `Discriminate`, `Representatives`, `Summaries` ([`4247a3b`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/4247a3b))
- `evaluate`: adjusted Rand index, normalized mutual information, purity, entropy, silhouette (seeded sampling above 10,000 rows), Davies–Bouldin and Calinski–Harabasz, cross-checked against scikit-learn ([`3b72ae2`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/3b72ae2))
- `cluto`: readers and writers for CLUTO sparse and dense matrices, label files and clustering files ([`3ae4669`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/3ae4669))
- `golden`: a harness that re-clusters CLUTO corpora and reports ARI, NMI and both partitions' I2; published numeric-only fixtures in `testdata/cluto` with an asserted agreement band ([`21be9cb`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/21be9cb))
- `clutro` CLI (`vcluster`, `evaluate`, `golden`) with CLUTO's flag vocabulary and standard-library flag parsing ([`5c07167`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/5c07167))
- Deterministic foundations: seeded named RNG sub-streams, a chunked worker pool with worker-order reductions, slow reference implementations the optimized paths are tested against, and a Determinism CI job across `GOMAXPROCS` values ([`e924afb`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/e924afb))
- Examples: sparse text with explanations, dense embeddings with internal metrics, choosing k from the bisection tree, the CLUTO file workflow, and density clustering via kinn-gg/hdbscan in a separate module ([`09b2b3c`](https://github.com/grokify/clutro/commit/09b2b3c))
